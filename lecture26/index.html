Gulab Jamun

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Milk Powder
  • Cardamom

Recipe

  1. Mix sugar and milk powder
  2. Add cardamom and shape into balls
  3. Fry until golden brown
  4. Soak in sugar syrup
